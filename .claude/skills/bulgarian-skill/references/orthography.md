## 1. The Alphabet and Phonetics

30 letters: А Б В Г Д Е Ж З И Й К Л М Н О П Р С Т У Ф Х Ц Ч Ш Щ Ъ Ь Ю Я

Key notes:
- **Ъ** (ер голям) represents the sound /ɤ/, similar to the "u" in English "but". It CANNOT appear at the end of a word in modern Bulgarian (since the 1945 reform). Where the sound occurs word-finally, it is written as **А**: бъркам but дъб → дъбът (with article) but "дъбове" (plural).
- **Ь** (ер малък) only appears before **О** to soften the preceding consonant: шофьор, актьор. It has no independent sound.
- The digraphs **дж** /dʒ/ and **дз** /dz/ are not separate letters but represent single sounds: джоб (pocket), дзън (clink).
- **Щ** represents /ʃt/: щастие (happiness), площад (square).

## 2. Слято, полуслято, разделно писане

This is arguably the hardest area of Bulgarian spelling. The rules determine whether compound words are written as one word, with a hyphen, or separately.

### Written as ONE word (слято):
- Compound nouns where the first part cannot stand alone or does not inflect independently: самолет, водопровод, земетресение
- Adverbs formed from preposition + noun/adjective: вляво, вдясно, впоследствие, наистина, предвид, отдясно
- Negated adjectives and adjectival participles: неправилен, нечетлив, необикновен

### Written with a HYPHEN (полуслято):
- Compounds where the second part takes the article: кандидат-кмет (кандидат-кметът), заместник-директор (заместник-директорът)
- Comparative and superlative prefixes: по-голям, най-добър
- Some established compound words: жар-птица, кръстословица is ONE word but каменно-въглищен has a hyphen

### Written SEPARATELY (разделно):
- Two-noun phrases where the FIRST noun inflects and takes the article: къща музей (къщата музей), лекар анестезиолог, художник символист
- Free word combinations: фитнес клуб, бизнес среща, рок група (common in practice but the rules can be ambiguous)

### The practical test
If the first word takes the article (and the second doesn't change), write them separately: "кокошката носачка" → кокошка носачка. If the second word takes the article, write with a hyphen: "заместник-директорът" → заместник-директор.

## 3. The Particle "не" — Joined or Separate

**SEPARATE** from verbs and verbal adverbs (-йки forms):
не знам, не сме, не искат, не знаейки, не искайки

**Exceptions** (written as one word): verbs that don't exist without "не": нехая, нехаейки; and with the prefix "недо-": недолюбвам.

**JOINED** with adjectives and adjectival participles:
неправилен, нечетлив, необикновен, непроверен

**JOINED** with nouns forming an antonym:
неистина, нещастие, несъгласие

## 4. Променливо "я" (Alternating Ya)

In some words, the letter **я** alternates with **е** depending on the syllable structure:

The letter **я** stays as **я** when:
- It is in a stressed syllable: бял, мляко, вяра
- It is followed by a soft syllable (one containing а, о, у, ъ, я): бяла, бяло

The letter **я** becomes **е** when:
- It is in an unstressed position before a hard syllable containing е or и: бели (not *бяли), млечен (not *млячен)
- голям → големи (not *голями)

This is one of the trickiest rules and affects adjectives, nouns, and verb forms.

## 5. Doubled Consonants

Doubled consonants occur at morpheme boundaries:
- от + там → оттам (from there)
- без + звучен → беззвучен (voiceless)
- рус + ски → руски (Russian) — note: NOT doubled in adjective suffixes -ски/-ски

In everyday speech the doubling is often not pronounced, which causes spelling errors. Pay attention to prefixes ending in the same letter that the root begins with: поддържам (support), оттеглям (withdraw), беззаконие (lawlessness).

## 6. Prepositions "във" and "със"

The prepositions "в" (in) and "с" (with) have extended forms:

- **в → във** before words starting with **в** or **ф**: във Варна, във фабриката
- **с → със** before words starting with **с** or **з**: със сол, със закуска

This also applies before numbers whose pronunciation starts with those sounds: във II век, със 100%.

The extended forms are also used when the preposition is stressed in contrast: със и без подготовка.

## 7. Punctuation

### Comma (запетая)
Bulgarian comma rules are syntactic, not prosodic (unlike English, where commas often follow pauses).

**Use a comma:**
- Before subordinating conjunctions: че, за да, защото, ако, когато, докато, тъй като, въпреки че, макар че, след като
- Before "който/която/което/които" and other relative pronouns
- To separate homogeneous parts: Купих ябълки, круши и банани.
- Before "а" (but/and — contrastive), "но" (but), "обаче" (however), "ала" (but — literary)
- After introductory words and phrases: Впрочем, ...; Разбира се, ...; За щастие, ...
- Around appositives: Иван, моят приятел, дойде.

**Do NOT use a comma:**
- Before "и" (and) in a simple list (use comma only before the last item if no "и")
- Before "или" in simple alternatives (unless they are clauses)
- Between subject and verb (a common error in Bulgarian!)

### Semicolon, colon, em-dash
Follow similar logic as in most European languages. The em-dash (тире) is used for parenthetical insertions and before direct speech attributions.

### Ellipsis
Three dots (...) with no space before them: Не знам...

## 8. Capitalization

**Capitalize:**
- The first word of a sentence
- Proper nouns (personal names, geographical names)
- The formal "Вие/Вас/Ви/Ваш" (even mid-sentence)
- Only the FIRST word of multi-word proper names of institutions, events, holidays: Европейска комисия (NOT *Европейска Комисия), Народно събрание, Нова година

**Do NOT capitalize:**
- Nationalities and languages when used as adjectives: български език, английски превод
- Days of the week and months: понеделник, януари
- The second and subsequent words of institutional names (unless themselves proper nouns): Софийски университет „Св. Климент Охридски"

## 9. Numbers and Formatting

### Decimal separator: COMMA (not period)
- 3,14 (NOT 3.14)

### Thousands separator: SPACE (not comma, not period)
- 1 000 000 (NOT 1,000,000)

### Currency
- Symbol AFTER the number with a space: 25 лв., 100 €, 53 200 EUR
- The word "евро" has no plural form in Bulgarian: 80 евро (NOT *80 евра)

### Dates
- Format: DD.MM.YYYY or written out: 15 април 2026 г.
- "г." = година (year)

### Phone numbers
Written in groups, typically: +359 888 123 456

## 10. Quotation Marks

Bulgarian uses two systems:
- **Traditional**: „ " (low-high, „Здравей")
- **Alternative**: " " (standard double quotes)

Either is acceptable if used consistently. Nested quotes use the other style: „Той каза: 'Ела тук'."

Punctuation placement:
- Inside the quotes if the quoted text is a complete independent sentence
- Outside if the quote is embedded in a larger sentence

---

## 11. Abbreviations

**After a consonant, put a period**: г. (година), бр. (брой), стр. (страница), проф. (професор)

**Initialism abbreviations**: lower case with periods, no spaces: т.е. (тоест), т.нар. (така наречен), напр. (например), и т.н. (и така нататък), и др. (и други)

**Acronyms from initials**: written in capitals without periods: БАН, ЕС, ООН

## 12. Common Spelling Errors Checklist

Before finalizing any Bulgarian text, check for:

1. ✅ Full vs. short article (see grammar reference)
2. ✅ "не" written separately from verbs, joined with adjectives
3. ✅ Compound words: слято/полуслято/разделно
4. ✅ Променливо "я" → "е" in unstressed position before е/и syllables
5. ✅ "във" before в/ф, "със" before с/з
6. ✅ No "ъ" at the end of words (use "а" instead)
7. ✅ Comma before "че", "който", "когато", "защото", "ако", etc.
8. ✅ No comma between subject and predicate
9. ✅ "ѝ" (to her) vs. "и" (and) — the grave accent matters
10. ✅ Numbers: comma for decimals, space for thousands
11. ✅ Capitalization: only first word of multi-word proper names
12. ✅ "-м" (not "-ме") for 1st person plural present tense
13. ✅ Hyphen with по- and най-: по-добър, най-висок
14. ✅ "предвид" (one word, NOT *"в предвид" or *"на предвид")
15. ✅ "вляво", "вдясно", "впоследствие" — written as one word
