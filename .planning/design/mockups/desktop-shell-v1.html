What I need from you

  1. Column width. 430 px (iPhone Pro Max width — the widest phone the mobile screens were drawn for) or 480 px. I recommend 430: the type scale was validated at 390 px, so a wider column gains nothing and risks re-wrapping.
  2. Outside the column. Flat base colour or a faint starfield. I recommend flat base — the column already carries the atmosphere, and a second background competes with it.
  3. Tab bar. Inside the column (as mobile) or a full-width strip with centred tabs. I recommend inside the column — a full-width bar puts five tabs under a 430 px column's empty gutters.
  4. Narrow screens. Below 430 px the column goes full-bleed with no side borders (shown in the 430 preview). Agreed?
  5. Out of scope for the shell. Legal pages, pricing and the sign-in / sign-up pages are text or Clerk pages. I plan to leave them full-width, not in the column, and let the footer stay where it is. Say if you want them inside too.
