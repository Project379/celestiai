The flow

  • 1 loading ember hero + Sun / Moon / Ascendant lighting with the stage lines (0 / 1.2 / 2.5 s), exactly oracle-loading-v2. Chart compute is ≈1 ms server-side, so the placements are known immediately; the wait is the horoscope.
  • 2 reveal new user only. Fills the horoscope wait instead of adding to it: each placement in turn grows and its label becomes the display line; a one-line trait sits under it. Skip is available from the first frame. Once ever.
  • 3 horoscope today's reading in the Оракул answer type (italic lead → body → Playfair payoff), the ember settles as the hero.
  • 4 chart one lit phrase + ember (the app's invitation device, never a boxed button), a real button: role, label, ≥48 px.

States shown

  • p50 / p90 / slow: horoscope arrives at 4.2 / 8.5 / 20 s. At ≥10 s the stage line becomes the "taking longer" line.
  • Horoscope failed (503, validation twice, or the edit-race 409): the horoscope slot becomes one quiet line and the chart button still appears — a failed horoscope never blocks the chart. Retry belongs to Днес (it already requests the horoscope).
  • Chart compute failed: the only state with a different button — one lit "try again" phrase instead of the chart button.
  • Reduced motion: glyphs static and lit, no fades/growth/twinkle/breathing, text and sections swap instantly; the reveal is the three labels shown at once with a Continue.

Accessibility

Stage text and the horoscope arrival are in a polite live region (the approved message is announced as the first stage line). Skip and the chart button are real buttons ≥48 px; skip is readable muted text, not decoration.
