Read as an object, not a moved card

hero Same moon-object build as Днес (three layered gradients + depth-twin + grain), sized for a detail screen (172px, between Днес's top hero and небесен ритъм's mini). No pill card wrapping it — the moon sits in open space the way it does everywhere else in this system.

title «Растяща луна» in the display face at 26px, starlight — this screen's own hero fact (the sky), same role dnes-v4's phase-mark plays, just larger since it IS the screen's subject here rather than a caption under a bigger hero.

fields Best-for / Affirmation / Crystal / Ritual are typographic fields, not cards — a small tracked bronze caption (matches the app's own SECTION_CAPTION_STYLE) directly above each body line, separated by a single hairline (the same device Ритъм's upcoming-row list already uses), never a bordered box.

exit journalPrompt + a lit «лунен дневник» line closes the screen — this screen's one exit, built from the Оракул ask-line device rather than a generic button, and it leads directly into artifact (c).

Old-direction fix: no pill boxes, no small cramped type, no stray rules — everything here traces to a real design-language device already ratified elsewhere in this system (moon build, section captions, hairline field separators, ask-line exit), not a port of LunarPhaseCard.tsx.
